## 常用习语与谚语

**1. pull one’s leg**  
开某人玩笑，逗某人。

- **【用法】**  
千万不要按照字面翻译成拉某人的腿，这是考试非常喜欢设置的干扰项。日常对话当中用来表示调侃、开玩笑。

- **【例句】**
  - Don’t worry, I’m just pulling your leg.
  - —You have won the national prize. —Stop pulling my leg. I know my real level.

**2. keep one’s fingers crossed**　  
祈求好运，祈祷一切顺利。

- **【用法】**  
考试、面试、比赛之前，为其他人送上祝福，盼望对方一切顺利。

- **【例句】**
  - I’ll keep my fingers crossed for your exam tomorrow.
  - We keep our fingers crossed that he can pass the job interview.

**3. cost an arm and a leg**
价格昂贵、贵得离谱。

- **【用法】**  
用来形容商品或者服务花销巨大，超出普通的消费水平。

- **【例句】**
  - This new phone cost me an arm and a leg.
  - That downtown apartment costs an arm and a leg for ordinary families.

**4. give someone the cold shoulder**   
冷落某人，不理睬某人。

- **【用法】**  
人际交往场景，指刻意疏远、冷淡对待另外一个人。

- **【例句】**
  - She gave me the cold shoulder at the party.
  - He gave his deskmate the cold shoulder after their quarrel.

**5. have a sweet tooth**　  
喜爱吃甜食。

- **【用法】**  
属于固定习语，这里的 tooth 不写成复数形式，不要按照字面生硬翻译。

- **【例句】**
  - I really have a sweet tooth; I can eat a whole cake.
  - Most kids have a sweet tooth and are fond of chocolate.

**6. keep an eye on**  
照看；密切注视。

- **【用法】**  
既可以用来表示照看人或者随身物品，也可以表达密切关注事态的发展变化。

- **【例句】**
  - Please keep an eye on my luggage while I buy a ticket.
  - The teacher keeps an eye on the progress of each group project.

**7. let the cat out of the bag**　  
泄露秘密，走漏风声。

- **【用法】**  
侧重在无意之间把原本需要保密的事情透露出去，spill the beans 是它的同义表达。

- **【例句】**
  - We were going to surprise her, but Tom let the cat out of the bag.
  - Don’t let the cat out of the bag before the award ceremony.

**8. kill two birds with one stone**  
一石二鸟，一举两得。

- **【用法】**  
描述做一件事情，同时收获两方面的好处。

- **【例句】**
  - By cycling to work, I kill two birds with one stone—I save money and get exercise.
  - Reading English novels can kill two birds with one stone: enrich vocabulary and improve reading skills.

**9. a dark horse**　  
黑马，出人意料的获胜者。

- **【用法】**  
竞赛、比拼的语境当中，用来指代一开始不被大家看好，最后脱颖而出取得好成绩的人。

- **【例句】**
  - He was a dark horse in the competition.
  - This unknown player turned out to be a dark horse in the contest.

**10. eat like a horse**  
食量很大，食量大如牛。

- **【用法】**  
专门用来形容人的饭量惊人，一般不用于描述动物。

- **【例句】**
  - He is very thin but he eats like a horse.
  - Though small-built, the athlete eats like a horse for energy supply.

**11. once in a blue moon**  
千载难逢，极少发生。

- **【用法】**  
用来描述某件事情发生的概率很低，平时很难遇到。

- **【例句】**
  - He only comes to visit us once in a blue moon.
  - Such a wonderful chance appears once in a blue moon.

**12. green with envy**　  
十分嫉妒，眼红。

- **【用法】**  
描述人的内心情绪，看到别人拥有好东西之后心生羡慕妒忌。

- **【例句】**
  - When she saw my new car, she was green with envy.
  - Her classmates were green with envy when she got the opportunity for further study.

**13. a white elephant**  
华而不实的东西。

- **【用法】**  
不要直译成白色大象，多指建筑、设备、工程项目，耗费大量财力，但是实际用处很小。

- **【例句】**
  - The new stadium is a white elephant.
  - That fancy machine turned out to be a white elephant for our small lab.

**14. in the black / in the red**  
盈利 / 亏损。

- **【用法】**  
商业、财务类阅读和单选高频短语，用来描述企业经营的财务状况。

- **【例句】**
  - The company has been in the red for two years.
  - After adjusting business strategies, the firm is finally in the black.

**15. spill the beans**  
泄露秘密。

- **【用法】**  
口语当中指无意把计划、秘密说了出去，和 let the cat out of the bag 含义接近。

- **【例句】**
  - Who spilled the beans about the party?
  - Don’t spill the beans about our travel plan.

**16. break the ice**  
打破僵局、破冰。

- **【用法】**  
社交场合，大家气氛尴尬陌生的时候，通过话语、小游戏消除隔阂，开启交流。

- **【例句】**
  - He told a joke to break the ice.
  - A small interesting game can help break the ice among new team members.

**17. hit the books**  
用功读书，埋头苦学。

- **【用法】**  
非正式口语，临近考试的时候，形容人抓紧时间刻苦学习。

- **【例句】**
  - I need to hit the books before the final exam.
  - All the students begin to hit the books as the examination draws near.

**18. under the weather**  
身体不适，有点生病。

- **【用法】**  
描述人的身体状态，身体有点不舒服，轻微生病。

- **【例句】**
  - I’m feeling a bit under the weather today.
  - She won’t attend the meeting because she is a little under the weather.

**19. out of the blue**  
突然地，出乎意料地。

- **【用法】**  
一件事情毫无预兆，突然之间发生。

- **【例句】**
  - He called me out of the blue yesterday.
  - The good news came out of the blue and excited the whole class.

**20. face the music**  
承担后果，接受惩罚。

- **【用法】**  
自己犯下错误之后，勇敢去面对、接受相应的处置，不要翻译成面对音乐。

- **【例句】**
  - You made a mistake, now you have to face the music.
  - Since you have broken the rule, you must face the music.

**21. That name rings a bell**  
这个名字听着耳熟。

- **【用法】**  
对人或者事物留有一点印象，但是回想不起完整的细节，句子后面常常搭配 but 进行转折。

- **【例句】**
  - —Have you ever heard of this awarded contemporary novelist? —That name rings a bell, yet I cannot recall his representative works.

**22. throw the baby out with the bathwater**  
因噎废食，全盘否定。

- **【用法】**  
告诫人们，不要因为一件事物存在缺点，就连同它本身具备的优点一并舍弃。

- **【例句】**
  - While restricting digital-device use in class, don’t throw the baby out with the bathwater, for modern technology also brings huge convenience to study.

**23. Rome was not built in a day**　  
罗马不是一天建成的。

- **【用法】**  
说明成就、能力都需要日积月累，做事情不能急于求成。

- **【例句】**
  - —I hope I can fully master this foreign language within only two months. —Rome was not built in a day. Persistent practice counts most.

**24. One man’s meat is another man’s poison**  
萝卜青菜，各有所爱。

- **【用法】**  
同样一件事物，不同的人喜好会完全不一样，不要把自己的喜好强加给其他人。

- **【例句】**
  - Don’t force other people to accept your personal hobby. One man’s meat is another man’s poison.
