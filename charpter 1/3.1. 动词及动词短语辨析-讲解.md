# 动词及动词短语辨析精讲

### 1. attract
**释义**：v. 吸引；引起（关注、兴趣）  
**用法要点**：及物动词，后面直接接宾语，不加介词。常用结构：attract sb.（吸引某人）；attract one’s attention / interest（引起某人注意、兴趣）；attract sb. to sth.（把某人吸引到某处）。主语通常是事物，靠自身特质产生吸引力。  
**例句**  
1. The unique local culture attracts plenty of tourists every year.  
2. The sudden noise attracted the guard’s attention immediately.  
**近义辨析**：draw 用法与 attract 高度重合，draw one’s attention 可替换；appeal to 意思接近，但 appeal 不及物，必须带 to，不能直接接人。  

### 2. differ
**释义**：v. 相异，有区别；（意见）不一致  
**用法要点**：不及物动词，不能直接跟宾语。differ in sth. 在某方面不同（in 后接比较的维度）；differ from sb./sth. 与某人某物不同（from 后接比较对象）；differ with sb. on/about sth. 在某事上与某人看法不一。  
**例句**  
1. The two cultures differ greatly in language and traditional customs.  
2. My idea differs from hers about how to organize the activity.  
**近义辨析**：be different from 是同义形容词结构，句法位置不同。  

### 3. reflect
**释义**：v. ① 反映，体现；② 倒映，映照；③ reflect on 回顾、反思  
**用法要点**：作“反映、倒映”时为及物动词，主语多为客观事物；reflect on 为固定短语，on 不可省略，后接需要复盘的内容，语气偏深度思考。  
**例句**  
1. A person’s behaviour often reflects his inner values.（反映）  
2. The smooth surface of the lake reflected the warm colours of the sunset.（倒映）  
3. After the competition, we reflected on what went well and what needed improvement.（反思）  
**近义辨析**：think about 泛指思考，语气平淡；consider 多指权衡是否采取某个行动。  

### 4. honour
**释义**：v. 尊敬；信守（承诺、协议）  
**用法要点**：及物动词，语体庄重。honour sb. 敬重某人；honour one’s promise / agreement 信守承诺、履行协议。  
**例句**  
1. We honour those who devoted themselves to public welfare.  
2. The company failed to honour its promise to improve working conditions.  
**近义辨析**：respect 日常通用，重在尊重他人；admire 侧重钦佩、欣赏他人的优点。  

### 5. decorate
**释义**：v. 装饰，装潢  
**用法要点**：及物动词，核心搭配 decorate sth. with sth.，在原有物体上添加饰物进行美化。  
**例句**  
1. We decorated the hall with fresh flowers and colourful lights for the ceremony.  
2. She likes to decorate her study with paintings she made herself.  
**近义辨析**：furnish 专指为房屋配备家具；ornament 偏书面，多指用小件饰物点缀。  

### 6. identify
**释义**：v. ① 识别，辨认，发现；② identify oneself with 认同  
**用法要点**：及物动词。identify sth. 识别某物；identify sb./sth. as sth. 确认为；identify oneself with sb./sth. 认同、产生共鸣。强调经判断、鉴定后确认目标。  
**例句**  
1. Scientists identified a previously unknown species in the deep ocean.（识别）  
2. Many young people identify themselves with the ideas in this book.（认同）  
**近义辨析**：recognize 侧重认出见过的人或物；discover 侧重发现原本存在却不为人知的事物。  

### 7. inspire
**释义**：v. 激励，鼓舞；赋予灵感  
**用法要点**：及物动词。inspire sb. to do sth. 激励某人做某事；inspire sth. in sb. 在某人心中激起某种情感。  
**例句**  
1. Her persistence inspired many young people to keep trying in difficulty.  
2. The quiet mountain scenery inspired the poet to write a new poem.  
**近义辨析**：encourage 侧重口头鼓励、打消顾虑；motivate 侧重激发内在动机。  

### 8. prevent
**释义**：v. 阻止，预防  
**用法要点**：及物动词。prevent sb. (from) doing sth. 阻止某人做某事，from 可省略；prevent sth. 预防不良事件。  
**例句**  
1. Strict safety rules help prevent accidents in the factory.  
2. Nothing can prevent him from achieving his long-term goal.  
**近义辨析**：stop 口语通用；forbid 侧重权威方明令禁止。  

### 9. suffer from
**释义**：短语：遭受（病痛、苦难、损失）  
**用法要点**：主语一般为人或生物，后接长期困扰的疾病、困境、灾害，侧重长期承受负面状态，不能表达获益。  
**例句**  
1. Quite a number of elderly people suffer from poor sleep quality.  
2. This area suffered from serious floods during last summer.  

### 10. recover from
**释义**：短语：从……中恢复（健康、打击、创伤）  
**用法要点**：主语多为人，宾语为疾病、创伤、打击等负面事件，强调逐步复原的过程。  
**例句**  
1. It took him several months to recover from the serious operation.  
2. She struggled to recover from the shock of losing her family member.  
**近义辨析**：get over 更口语，可指病愈，也可指克服心理难关。  

### 11. benefit from
**释义**：短语：从……当中获益  
**用法要点**：主语是人。另需掌握 sth. benefit sb.，benefit 作及物动词直接接宾语，不加 to；benefit 习惯上不用被动形式，不说 are benefited。  
**例句**  
1. The company took measures to make sure workers could benefit from the new training programme.  
2. Students benefit a lot from regular reading practice.  

### 12. separate from
**释义**：短语：与……分离，分开  
**用法要点**：separate A from B 把 A 与 B 分开；be separated from 被与……隔开，多用于原本相连或同处的事物被拆开。  
**例句**  
1. A stone wall separates the garden from the main road.  
2. The little girl was separated from her parents in the crowded square.  
**近义辨析**：divide into 指把整体划分为若干部分。  

### 13. belong to
**释义**：短语：属于  
**用法要点**：语法限制明确——无被动语态，也不用进行时，不说 is belonged to、is belonging to。主语是物，后接所有者。  
**例句**  
1. This old wooden box belongs to my grandfather.  
2. These cultural relics belong to the whole nation.  

### 14. prefer to
**释义**：短语：更喜欢；宁愿  
**用法要点**：prefer to do sth. 宁愿做某事；prefer A to B 比起 B 更喜欢 A，此结构中 to 为介词，后接名词或动名词；prefer to do A rather than do B 宁愿做 A 而不做 B。  
**例句**  
1. I prefer to stay at home on cold rainy days.  
2. She prefers walking to taking buses on sunny mornings.  
**近义辨析**：would rather 后接动词原形，侧重主观选择。  

### 15. look forward to
**释义**：短语：期待，盼望  
**用法要点**：to 为介词，后面只能接名词或动名词 doing，不能接动词原形。  
**例句**  
1. All the students are looking forward to the coming winter holiday.  
2. He looks forward to visiting the ancient city again.  

### 16. get used to
**释义**：短语：习惯于  
**用法要点**：to 为介词，后接名词或动名词 doing。需与 used to do（过去常常，现在不再）、be used to do（被用来做）区分。  
**例句**  
1. It took her nearly a month to get used to getting up at six every morning.  
2. You will get used to the pace of life here soon.  

### 17. complain about
**释义**：短语：抱怨  
**用法要点**：不及物短语，不能直接接宾语。complain about sth. 抱怨某事；complain to sb. about sth. 向某人抱怨某事。  
**例句**  
1. Many citizens complain about heavy traffic jams during rush hour.  
2. It is useless to complain about small troubles all the time.  

### 18. rather than
**释义**：短语：而不是  
**用法要点**：连接平行结构，前后成分形式一致，可连接名词、动词原形、动名词；常与 prefer、would do 搭配，表示取舍对比。  
**例句**  
1. We should follow the traffic rules rather than complain about traffic jams.  
2. I choose to cycle rather than take the crowded subway.  
**近义辨析**：instead of 侧重代替，后面常接动名词。  

### 19. work out
**释义**：短语：① 算出；② 解决；③ 进展顺利  
**用法要点**：作及物短语时接 problem、puzzle 等，表示算出、解决；作不及物短语时主语为事情、计划，表示结果良好。  
**例句**  
1. It took me half an hour to work out this difficult math problem.（算出）  
2. They worked out a plan to reduce costs together.（解决）  
3. I hope everything will work out well in the end.（进展顺利）  
**近义辨析**：solve 为及物动词，直接接 problem，重在解决问题本身。  

### 20. burn up
**释义**：短语：烧毁，烧尽  
**用法要点**：多指物体被烧成灰烬。  
**例句**  
1. All the dry grass burned up in the sudden big fire.  
2. Those old letters burned up in the accident last year.  
**近义辨析**：burn down 多指建筑物被烧毁、坍塌。  

### 21. catch up
**释义**：短语：赶上，追上  
**用法要点**：catch up with sb./sth. 追上某人、赶上进度；catch up on sth. 补做落下的功课或工作。前提是原本落后。  
**例句**  
1. He ran as fast as he could and soon caught up with the other runners.  
2. I need to catch up on the homework I missed because of illness.  
**近义辨析**：keep up with 侧重一直跟上节奏、不落后。  

### 22. depend on
**释义**：短语：依靠，依赖；取决于  
**用法要点**：接人时表示依靠，接事物时表示结果由该条件决定。  
**例句**  
1. Whether we go hiking depends on tomorrow’s weather.（取决于）  
2. Little kids depend on their parents for care and support.（依靠）  
**近义辨析**：rely on 更强调信任、信赖。  

### 23. wind up
**释义**：短语：① 结束，收尾；② wind up doing 到头来、最终做某事  
**用法要点**：表结束时多用于会议、讨论等正式场景；wind up doing 强调最终形成的结果，常带意外感。  
**例句**  
1. Since we have reached an agreement, we decide to wind up the meeting.（收尾）  
2. If you keep making careless mistakes, you may wind up getting punished.（到头来）  
**近义辨析**：end up doing 同义，日常使用频率更高。  

### 24. maintain
**释义**：v. 维护，保养；维持；坚持认为  
**用法要点**：及物动词。maintain sth. 保养设施、维持关系或秩序；maintain that… 坚持认为某观点成立。  
**例句**  
1. Local villagers use modern methods to maintain the Longji Rice Terraces.（保养）  
2. It is hard to maintain a close friendship over a long distance.（维持）  
3. He maintained that he had never lied to anyone.（坚持认为）  
**近义辨析**：preserve 侧重保护，防止文物、自然事物损坏消亡；keep 为通用用词。  

### 25. deliver
**释义**：v. 递送；发表（演讲、报告）  
**用法要点**：及物动词。deliver sth. to sb. 把某物递送给某人；deliver a speech / report 发表演讲、报告。  
**例句**  
1. The postman delivers newspapers to every household early in the morning.（递送）  
2. She will deliver a speech about environmental protection at the meeting.（发表）  
**近义辨析**：send 泛指发送，适用范围更广。  

### 26. wave
**释义**：v. 挥手；起伏，波动  
**用法要点**：wave to sb. 向某人挥手；wave sth. 挥动某物；描述植物、水面时表示起伏摇晃。  
**例句**  
1. He waved to me warmly when he saw me across the street.（挥手）  
2. The golden wheat waves gently in the wind.（起伏）  

### 27. wrap
**释义**：v. 包裹；缠绕  
**用法要点**：wrap sth. in sth. 用某物包裹；wrap sth. around sth. 缠绕在……上，多指用纸、布料包裹物品。  
**例句**  
1. She wrapped the birthday gift in shiny paper.（包裹）  
2. The nurse wrapped the bandage around his injured arm.（缠绕）  
**近义辨析**：pack 侧重装箱打包。  

### 28. break down
**释义**：短语：①（机器、车辆）出故障，抛锚；②（情绪）崩溃；③ 分解  
**用法要点**：不及物短语，无被动。主语可为交通工具、机器，也可为人；化学语境下表示分解。  
**例句**  
1. Her car broke down on the way, so I offered her a ride.（抛锚）  
2. He broke down when he heard the sad news.（情绪崩溃）  
3. This kind of material breaks down slowly in nature.（分解）  

### 29. give in
**释义**：短语：屈服，让步  
**用法要点**：give in to sb./sth. 向某人、压力或诱惑屈服。  
**例句**  
1. We should not give in to difficulties easily.  
2. He refused to give in to their unreasonable demands.  

### 30. show up
**释义**：短语：出现，露面  
**用法要点**：不及物短语，多指人到场、现身。  
**例句**  
1. No one showed up for the morning meeting on time.  
2. We waited for two hours, but he never showed up.  
**近义辨析**：turn up 既指人露面，也可指物品被找到、音量调大。  

### 31. get across
**释义**：短语：讲清楚，使（观点）被理解  
**用法要点**：get sth. across to sb.，把想法传达给别人并让对方听懂。  
**例句**  
1. It is difficult to get my idea across with simple English words.  
2. The speaker failed to get his main point across to the audience.  

### 32. resist
**释义**：v. 抵抗；忍住；抗拒  
**用法要点**：can / could not resist doing sth. 忍不住做某事，resist 后只能接名词或动名词，不能接不定式。  
**例句**  
1. The little boy could not resist playing outside after school.  
2. I can hardly resist eating chocolate when I see it.  

### 33. pass down
**释义**：短语：代代相传，传承  
**用法要点**：及物短语，宾语多为传统、技艺、故事等抽象事物，侧重时间维度上由上一代传给下一代。  
**例句**  
1. This ancient tradition has been passed down from generation to generation.  
2. The special skill is passed down within the family.  

### 34. pass by
**释义**：短语：经过；忽视，不理会  
**用法要点**：可指人、车辆路过，也可引申为错过机会、无视。  
**例句**  
1. A group of cyclists passed by our house at noon.（经过）  
2. Don’t pass by this valuable chance.（忽视）  

### 35. put up
**释义**：短语：张贴；搭建；留宿  
**用法要点**：put up a notice 张贴通知；put up a tent 搭建帐篷；put sb. up 留某人住宿。  
**例句**  
1. They put up a notice about the sports meeting on the wall.（张贴）  
2. We put up a tent beside the lake for the night.（搭建）  

### 36. put down
**释义**：短语：放下；写下；镇压  
**例句**  
1. Please put down your pen and listen carefully.（放下）  
2. He put down all the key points in his notebook.（写下）  

### 37. measure
**释义**：v. 测量；估量  
**用法要点**：及物动词，测量具体尺寸；也可用于抽象衡量。  
**例句**  
1. We measured the width and length of the wooden table.（测量）  
2. True success cannot be measured only by money.（估量）  

### 38. impress
**释义**：v. 使印象深刻  
**用法要点**：impress sb.；被动结构 be impressed by / with sth.。  
**例句**  
1. Her courage and calm impressed all the people present.  
2. I was deeply impressed by her wonderful performance.  

### 39. pour
**释义**：v. 倾倒；倾泻；（大雨）倾盆而下  
**用法要点**：及物时后接液体；不及物时用于描述降雨。  
**例句**  
1. She poured hot tea into the cup slowly.（倾倒）  
2. It poured all night and the road was wet everywhere.（下雨）  

### 40. observe
**释义**：v. ① 观察；② 遵守；③ 庆祝、奉行（节日）  
**用法要点**：表观察时带有目的性与系统性；表遵守时搭配规则、法律；节日义项偏正式书面，被动句式常见。  
**例句**  
1. Scientists observe the behaviour of wild birds for research.（观察）  
2. Everyone must observe the school rules.（遵守）  
3. The Dragon Boat Festival is observed on the fifth day of the fifth lunar month.（庆祝）  
**近义辨析**：celebrate 为通用词，直接表示庆祝节日。  

### 41. admire
**释义**：v. 赞赏，羡慕；欣赏  
**用法要点**：admire sb. for sth. 因某事钦佩某人，侧重发自内心欣赏他人的品质或成果。  
**例句**  
1. Neighbours stopped to admire the beautiful garden he built.  
2. I admire her for her perseverance in hard times.  
**近义辨析**：praise 侧重口头表扬。  

### 42. appreciate
**释义**：v. 欣赏；感激；理解  
**用法要点**：表感激时后接事物，不接人；表欣赏时强调看懂事物内在价值。  
**例句**  
1. We appreciate your timely help during the difficulty.（感激）  
2. It is hard to appreciate this painting without knowing its background story.（欣赏）  

### 43. postpone
**释义**：v. 推迟  
**用法要点**：postpone sth. to / until sth.，多用于计划、活动。  
**例句**  
1. They had to postpone the sports meeting because of heavy rain.  
2. The trip was postponed until next month.  
**近义辨析**：put off 为口语同义短语。  

### 44. end up doing
**释义**：短语：最终（落得）做某事，到头来……  
**用法要点**：固定搭配，后接动名词 doing，常暗含结果与最初设想不同。  
**例句**  
1. I planned to finish my work early, but I ended up doing all the cleaning myself.  
2. If you keep telling lies, you will end up losing all your friends.  

### 45. pick up
**释义**：短语：① 捡起；② 接人；③（偶然）学会；④ 好转；⑤ 接收信号  
**例句**  
1. I picked up a wallet on my way to school this morning.（捡起）  
2. She will pick up her sister at the railway station.（接人）  
3. He picked up Spanish while working in Madrid.（学会）  
4. Her health picked up after a long rest.（好转）  

### 46. make up
**释义**：短语：① 编造（故事、借口）；② 弥补；③ 化妆；④ 组成  
**例句**  
1. I am not making up a story. Everything I told you is true.（编造）  
2. She made up for her mistake by working extra hours.（弥补）  
3. Girls make up before going on stage.（化妆）  
4. These small parts make up the whole machine.（组成）  

### 47. turn up
**释义**：短语：① 出现，露面；② 调大音量；③（丢失物品）被找到  
**例句**  
1. My missing keys turned up under the sofa finally.（被找到）  
2. Please turn up the radio a little; I can hardly hear it.（调大）  

### 48. dress up
**释义**：短语：打扮；盛装  
**用法要点**：dress up as sb./sth. 装扮成某人某物。  
**例句**  
1. The children dressed up as animals for the party.（装扮）  
2. You do not need to dress up for this simple meeting.（盛装）  

### 49. picture
**释义**：v. 想象，设想（名词义：图片）  
**用法要点**：picture sth. / picture doing sth.，在脑海中形成画面式的想象，属熟词僻义。  
**例句**  
1. She had pictured this moment over and over again in her mind.  
2. Try to picture living beside a quiet lake.  
**近义辨析**：imagine 为通用想象。  

### 50. explore
**释义**：v. 探索；考察；探究  
**用法要点**：及物动词，可搭配地点，也可搭配方法、可能性等抽象名词。  
**例句**  
1. They set off to explore the thick forest.（探索）  
2. We need to explore new ways to solve this problem.（探究）  

### 51. investigate
**释义**：v. 调查  
**用法要点**：多用于事故、案件、问题，侧重查明真相。  
**例句**  
1. The police are investigating the cause of the traffic accident.  
2. The team will investigate why the plan failed.
