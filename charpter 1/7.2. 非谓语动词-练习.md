# 非谓语动词专项训练题（试题卷）

> 说明：共 120 题，单项选择 60 题 + 语法填空 60 题；覆盖非谓语识别、不定式、动名词、分词、分词形容词及相对时态语态判断。  
> 语法填空：根据括号内动词原形，填写正确的非谓语形式。

## 第一部分 单项选择（60 题）

1. ______ by his parents, the young man decided to give up the boring job.  
A. Encouraging  B. Encouraged  C. To encourage  D. Having encouraged

2. To help us prepare for the final exam, the teacher suggested ______ our notes every evening.  
A. review  B. to review  C. reviewing  D. reviewed

3. He hurried to the station, only ______ that the train had left ten minutes earlier.  
A. find  B. finding  C. to find  D. found

4. The building ______ next month will serve as our new school library.  
A. completing  B. completed  C. to be completed  D. having completed

5. The chair is comfortable ______, so I want to buy one like it.  
A. to sit  B. to sit on  C. sitting  D. sitting on

6. ______ in his favourite novel, he didn’t hear my call at all.  
A. Absorbing  B. Absorbed  C. To absorb  D. Having absorbed

7. He pretended ______ when his teacher suddenly stepped into the classroom.  
A. read  B. reading  C. to be reading  D. to have read

8. He seems ______ the secret, for he keeps silent all the time.  
A. to know  B. to have known  C. knowing  D. having known

9. He narrowly escaped ______ in the terrible traffic accident last week.  
A. killing  B. to kill  C. being killed  D. to be killed

10. ______ up in a small town, I am used to the quiet life here.  
A. Grow  B. Growing  C. Grown  D. To grow

11. Many old companies, ______ their fame for centuries, are now facing hard challenges.  
A. building  B. built  C. having built  D. to build

12. She raised her voice so that she could make herself ______ by everyone in the hall.  
A. hear  B. hearing  C. heard  D. to hear

13. The English ______ in that area is quite different from what we learn in textbooks.  
A. speaking  B. spoken  C. to speak  D. having spoken

14. She has the ability ______ creative ideas in every class activity.  
A. produce  B. producing  C. to produce  D. produced

15. He works hard every day so as ______ the important exam in June.
