# 英语长难句剥洋葱专项训练100题


### 第1题

原句：While those who argue that technological progress will automatically free human beings from repetitive labour often overlook the reality that new forms of work, which demand creative thinking and emotional judgment, will constantly emerge to replace the positions that machines have taken over, many sociologists insist that the social adjustment required to balance technological advantages and human value will be far more complex than most ordinary people expect.  
来源：剑桥雅思人文篇章改编（高考培优适配）

**① 洋葱核：** many sociologists insist that the social adjustment will be far more complex.

**② 洋葱皮解析：**
1. While those…taken over：【让步状语从句】，由 While 引导，修饰整个主句，交代对比背景；  
2. who argue that…repetitive labour：【定语从句】，修饰 those；  
3. that technological progress…repetitive labour：【宾语从句】，作 argue 的宾语；  
4. that new forms of work…taken over：【同位语从句】，解释 reality 的具体内容；  
5. which demand creative thinking and emotional judgment：【非限制性定语从句】，修饰 new forms of work；  
6. that machines have taken over：【定语从句】，修饰 positions；  
7. required to balance technological advantages and human value：【过去分词短语作后置定语】，修饰 the social adjustment；  
8. than most ordinary people expect：【比较状语从句】，修饰 more complex。

**③ 译文：** 那些主张技术进步会自动把人类从重复劳动中解放出来的人，往往忽视了这样一个现实：新的工作形态会不断涌现，以取代机器已经接管的岗位，而这些新工作需要创造性思维与情感判断；许多社会学家坚持认为，要在技术优势与人的价值之间取得平衡，所需的社会调整远比多数普通人想象的复杂。
