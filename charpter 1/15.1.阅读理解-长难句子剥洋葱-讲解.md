# 英语长难句专项讲解

## 第一部分：长难句拆解——剥洋葱

### 一、面对复杂长难句，我们该怎么办？

很多同学在读阅读、完形里的长句子时，经常一句话看完，单词基本认识，但整句话意思抓不住。  
我们先看一句典型、有压迫感的高考难度长难句：

> He argued that if we ignore the damage human activities have done to the ocean, we will soon find that many marine species that once thrived in these waters may disappear forever.

第一眼看上去：从句套从句，一层叠一层，信息挤在一起，很难快速抓住重点。  
这类句子的难点，不在于生词，而在于信息堆叠。  
我们需要一套可复制、可反复练习的拆解工具，这套工具就是**剥洋葱法**。  
剥洋葱法的核心思路：长难句不是杂乱拼接，它由【洋葱核（句子主干骨架）】和层层包裹在外的【洋葱皮（各类修饰补充信息）】组成。  
洋葱核是句子必不可少的骨架，去掉所有外皮之后，依然是语法成立的基础结构；洋葱皮负责补充背景、限定对象、追加信息，不能独立充当主句骨架。  
完整操作三步：
1. 剥：识别全部洋葱皮，临时剥离，提取洋葱核；
2. 析：逐一判断每一块洋葱皮修饰对象、语法功能；
3. 装：从内向外，逐层把洋葱皮装回主干，逐步读懂完整句意。

### 二、洋葱核：句子主干的三大基础句型

所有英语主句主干，都归属下面三类基础骨架，这是判断“核”的标准。

#### 句型1：主谓宾（S+V+O）

结构：主语 + 及物动词 + 宾语  
含义：主语发出动作，动作作用于宾语。  
例句：The researcher recorded the data.  
译文：这名研究员记录下数据。  
- The researcher（主语），recorded（谓语动词），the data（宾语），这就是洋葱核。

#### 句型2：主系表（S+V+P）

结构：主语 + 系动词 + 表语  
含义：描述主语的身份、性质、状态。  
常见系动词：be 动词；感官动词 look/sound/smell/taste/feel；变化类 become/get/turn/grow；保持类 keep/stay/remain 等。  
例句：The conclusion remains controversial.  
译文：这个结论仍然存在争议。  
- The conclusion（主语），remains（系动词），controversial（表语），洋葱核。

#### 句型3：There be 存在句型

结构：There be + 真正主语  
含义：表达“某地/某时存在某物”。  
例句：There is a hidden risk.  
译文：存在一个隐藏的风险。  
- There be 为固定框架，a hidden risk 是真正主语，共同构成洋葱核。

> 重要提醒：
> 一个主句，**只拥有一套主句谓语（带时态变化的动词）**。
> 从句内部自带的谓语、非谓语动词（to do / doing / done），不属于主句谓语，绝大多数情况属于洋葱皮。

### 三、洋葱皮全分类：识别标志

洋葱皮就是附加信息。除基础类型之外，这里补充嵌套从句、并列同类从句、多层定语、分隔修饰等高考高频复杂结构，全部配有中等偏难例句。

#### 1. 状语从句（洋葱皮）

识别引导词：although, though, if, when, while, as, because, since, until, unless, where, once, even if, provided that 等。  
特征：引导词开头，自带一套主谓，交代时间、条件、原因、让步、地点、方式等背景。  
处理方式：从引导词开始，直到这个从句结束，整块剥离。  
例句：Even if we raise public awareness continuously, we cannot solve the environmental problem overnight.  
识别：Even if we raise public awareness continuously 让步状语从句（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：we cannot solve the environmental problem overnight.  
主干译文：我们无法一夜之间解决环境问题。

#### 2. 定语从句（洋葱皮）

识别关系词：who, whom, whose, which, that, where, when, why。  
特征：紧跟名词之后，限定、修饰前面名词；高考常考**双层嵌套定语从句**。  
处理方式：从关系词起，到从句结尾，整块剥离；多层嵌套由外向内逐层剥。  
嵌套难度例句：The theory that the professor put forward which challenges traditional views has caused heated debate.  
识别：that the professor put forward、which challenges traditional views 两层定语从句，共同修饰 the theory（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The theory has caused heated debate.  
主干译文：这个理论引发了激烈讨论。

#### 3. 宾语从句（洋葱皮）

识别引导词：that, whether, if, what, who, where, when, how, why 等。  
特征：放在及物动词或者介词后面，充当动词、介词的宾语。  
高频难点：**并列双宾语从句 / 多个宾语从句并列**，一个动词后面连续带两个及以上宾语从句。  
处理方式：引导词到从句末尾，整块剥离；并列从句统一作为一层洋葱皮。  
并列宾语从句例句：Many experts believe that small habits shape one’s character and that character finally decides one’s future.  
识别：that small habits shape one’s character 和 that character finally decides one’s future 是由 and 连接的两个并列宾语从句，共同作 believe 的宾语（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：Many experts believe.  
主干译文：很多专家认为。

> 补充难点：动词后既有宾语从句，又接其他附加修饰，很容易混淆。  
例句：She reminded us that the deadline had been changed and that we should hand in our report before Friday.  
识别：两个 that 引导并列宾语从句（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：She reminded us.  
主干译文：她提醒了我们。

#### 4. 表语从句（洋葱皮）

识别引导词：that, whether, what, why, where, how 等。  
特征：放在系动词（be / seem / become / remain / prove 等）之后。  
难点拓展：并列表语从句。  
例句：The truth is that few people realize the value of quiet reflection and that constant noise weakens our ability to think deeply.  
识别：两个 that 引导并列表语从句（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The truth is.  
主干译文：事实是。

#### 5. 同位语从句（洋葱皮，极易和定语从句混淆）

识别标志：跟在抽象名词后（fact, news, idea, belief, doubt, promise, evidence），多用 that 引导，用来解释名词本身内容，不是限定修饰。  
处理方式：同位语从句整块剥离。  
例句：The evidence that global warming speeds up glacier melting has been accepted by most scientists.  
识别：that global warming speeds up glacier melting 同位语从句，解释 evidence 的内容（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The evidence has been accepted by most scientists.  
主干译文：这份证据已经被大多数科学家认可。

#### 6. 非谓语短语：doing / done / to do（洋葱皮）

特征：没有独立主语，没有时态变化，不能充当主句谓语；放在名词后常作定语，句首/句末常作状语。  
难点拓展：多个非谓语叠加、非谓语自带自己的宾语和修饰。  
例句：Having analyzed masses of collected data, the team found a pattern hidden inside the social phenomenon.  
识别：Having analyzed masses of collected data 现在分词作状语；hidden inside the social phenomenon 过去分词短语后置定语修饰 pattern（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：the team found a pattern.  
主干译文：这个团队发现了一种规律。

例句：To fully understand cultural differences, we need to communicate with local residents actively.  
识别：To fully understand cultural differences 不定式作目的状语（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：we need to communicate with local residents actively.  
主干译文：我们需要主动和当地居民交流。

#### 7. 介词短语（洋葱皮）

识别标志：in, on, at, for, with, from, like, despite, beyond, during, regarding, concerning 等介词开头 + 名词结构。  
功能：可作定语、状语；难点：长串连续介词叠加修饰。  
例句：The research into the influence of social media on teenagers’ mental health received wide public attention.  
识别：into the influence of social media on teenagers’ mental health 多层嵌套介词短语后置修饰 research（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The research received wide public attention.  
主干译文：这项研究获得广泛关注。

#### 8. 插入语（洋葱皮）

识别标志：成对逗号、破折号隔开；常见：I think, I suppose, in fact, for example, to be honest, as far as I know, what’s more。  
特征：插在句子中间，拿掉之后主句语法完整，主干不受影响；难点：插入语插在从句中间，干扰视线。  
例句：The discovery, scientists believe, will rewrite our understanding of ancient life on this planet.  
识别：scientists believe 插入语（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The discovery will rewrite our understanding of ancient life on this planet.  
主干译文：这项发现将改写我们对地球上古生物的认知。

#### 9. 同位语（名词性同位语，洋葱皮）

识别标志：紧跟名词之后，逗号隔开，对等解释前面名词。  
例句：His goal, a balanced development of economy and environment, guides all the local policies.  
识别：a balanced development of economy and environment 同位语，解释 his goal（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：His goal guides all the local policies.  
主干译文：他的目标指导着所有地方政策。

#### 10. 比较结构（洋葱皮）

识别标志：than, as…as 引导比较部分，修饰形容词、副词。  
难点：比较结构里面还可以嵌套从句。  
嵌套比较例句：This method works more effectively than the one we used last semester.  
识别：than the one we used last semester 比较状语，内部还嵌套定语从句 we used last semester（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：This method works more effectively.  
主干译文：这个方法效果更好。

#### 11. 分隔修饰结构（高考高频难点洋葱皮）

识别标志：主语和谓语之间被一长串修饰隔开，主语很远才能找到谓语，很容易看错主干。  
例句：The collection of ancient paintings handed down from the Tang Dynasty, which contains more than two hundred works, is displayed in the national museum.  
识别：of ancient paintings handed down from the Tang Dynasty、which contains more than two hundred works，夹在主语 the collection 和谓语 is displayed 之间，属于分隔修饰（洋葱皮）。  
剥离后洋葱核：The collection is displayed in the national museum.  
主干译文：这批藏品在国家博物馆展出。

#### 12. 并列分句（and / but / while 连接独立分句）

识别标志：并列连词连接两套独立主谓结构；当我们锁定其中一套作为主句洋葱核时，另外一套并列分句整体视为洋葱皮。  
例句：Many people admire his achievements, but few realize the price he paid to get them.  
识别：but 引导后面并列分句，若把前半句作为主句，后半句就是洋葱皮；反之同理。  
剥离后洋葱核：Many people admire his achievements.  
主干译文：很多人羡慕他的成就。

### 四、综合识别小练习

1. He warned us that sudden climate change would threaten coastal cities and that we should take preventive measures in advance.  
> 提示：两个 that 引导并列宾语从句，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：He warned us.

2. The idea that humans can live in harmony with nature is something that every citizen should keep in mind.  
> 提示：that humans can live in harmony with nature 同位语从句；that every citizen should keep in mind 定语从句，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：The idea is something.

3. If we keep wasting limited natural resources, we will face a situation where future generations can hardly satisfy their basic needs.  
> 提示：if 引导状语从句；where 引导定语从句修饰 situation，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：we will face a situation.

4. The report released by the research centre shows that air quality has improved greatly in those industrial zones.  
> 提示：released by the research centre 过去分词定语；that 引导宾语从句，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：The report shows.

5. The fact, as many researchers have pointed out, cannot be ignored by policy makers.  
> 提示：as many researchers have pointed out 插入语，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：The fact cannot be ignored by policy makers.

6. There exists a widespread view that constant online communication reduces face-to-face interaction among young people.  
> 提示：that 引导同位语从句；among young people 介词短语，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：There exists a widespread view.

7. The writer created a story which reflects social pressure and which encourages readers to stick to their own dreams.  
> 提示：两个 which 并列定语从句修饰 story，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：The writer created a story.

8. Having observed animal behaviour for ten years, the biologist found that some species have much higher intelligence than people once supposed.  
> 提示：Having observed…分词状语；that 宾语从句；than 比较结构，洋葱皮  
> 洋葱核：the biologist found.

### 五、剥洋葱避坑清单

1. 剥离洋葱皮的时候，不要改动洋葱核里面任何单词，避免主干提取错误。
2. 从句、并列分句都有自己的主谓结构，但只要不属于主句骨架，就属于洋葱皮。
3. to do / doing / done 绝大多数情况是非谓语，属于洋葱皮，不要误判为主句谓语。
4. 宾语从句、表语从句剥离之后主干很短、语义不完整是正常现象；剥离只是找骨架手段，理解句意必须回装。
5. 遇到分隔修饰、多层嵌套、并列从句：优先从外层洋葱皮下手，一层层向内剥，不要一次性全部摘除。
6. 区分同位语从句和定语从句：同位语从句解释名词内容；定语从句限定名词范围。二者都属于洋葱皮，识别出来剥离即可。
7. 并列宾语从句、并列表语从句经常共用一个引导词，第二个引导词 that 有时候会省略，要留意识别。


## 第二部分：剥洋葱实战案例集（30 句高难长难句拆解）

> 统一模板：  
> 原句  
> ①剥洋葱：剔除全部洋葱皮，提取洋葱核  
> ②洋葱皮解析：逐条说明每一块修饰成分、修饰对象、语法功能
> ③重新组装洋葱：由内向外逐层添加修饰，还原理解句意

### 案例1

真题原句：  
Although AI robots do pretty well in things requiring high-level reasoning like math, they perform worse than a one-year-old child when it comes to simple tasks demanding abilities of emotion, reflexes and mobility.

①剥洋葱：剔除全部洋葱皮，提取洋葱核  
剔除内容：Although 引导的让步状语从句；than a one-year-old child 比较状语；when 引导的状语从句  
洋葱核：they perform worse.  
主干译文：它们表现更差。

②洋葱皮解析  
1. Although AI robots do pretty well in things requiring high-level reasoning like math：让步状语从句，交代转折背景；requiring high-level reasoning 现在分词后置定语，修饰 things；like math 介词短语举例，修饰 high-level reasoning。  
2. than a one-year-old child：比较状语，修饰副词 worse，交代比较对象。  
3. when it comes to simple tasks demanding abilities of emotion, reflexes and mobility：条件状语从句；demanding abilities of emotion, reflexes and mobility 现在分词后置定语，修饰 simple tasks。

③重新组装洋葱  
第一层（主干）：they perform worse.  
第二层（两大状语框架）：Although AI robots do pretty well in things, they perform worse when it comes to simple tasks.  
译文：尽管人工智能机器人在一些事情上表现不错，但当涉及简单任务时，它们表现更差。  
第三层（添加分词定语）：Although AI robots do pretty well in things requiring high-level reasoning, they perform worse when it comes to simple tasks demanding abilities of emotion, reflexes and mobility.  
译文：尽管人工智能机器人在需要高阶推理的事情上表现不错，但在需要情感、反应与行动能力的简单任务上，它们表现甚至不如一岁孩童。  
第四层（添加举例、比较对象，还原原句）：Although AI robots do pretty well in things requiring high-level reasoning like math, they perform worse than a one-year-old child when it comes to simple tasks demanding abilities of emotion, reflexes and mobility.  
完整译文：尽管人工智能机器人在数学这类需要高阶推理的事情上表现优异，但在需要情感、反应与行动能力的简单任务上，它们表现甚至不如一岁孩童。

### 案例2

真题原句：  
Nature offers its wonders not to those who are in a hurry, but to those who are patient and truly ready to receive them.

①剥洋葱：剔除全部洋葱皮，提取洋葱核  
剔除内容：not to those… but to those… 并列介词短语；两处 who 引导定语从句  
洋葱核：Nature offers its wonders.  
主干译文：大自然馈赠奇观。

②洋葱皮解析  
1. not to those who are in a hurry, but to those who are patient and truly ready to receive them：并列介词短语作状语，not…but… 构成对比；who are in a hurry 定语从句修饰第一个 those；who are patient and truly ready to receive them 定语从句修饰第二个 those。

③重新组装洋葱  
第一层（主干）：Nature offers its wonders.  
第二层（添加对比状语框架）：Nature offers its wonders not to those, but to those.  
译文：大自然的奇观不会赠予一类人，而是赠予另一类人。  
第三层（添加两处定语从句，还原原句）：Nature offers its wonders not to those who are in a hurry, but to those who are patient and truly ready to receive them.  
完整译文：大自然不会把奇观赠予匆匆赶路之人，而会留给那些有耐心、真心准备好接纳它的人。

### 案例3

真题原句：  
He argued that if we ignore the damage human activities have done to the ocean, we will soon find that many marine species that once thrived in these waters may disappear forever.

①剥洋葱：剔除全部洋葱皮，提取洋葱核  
剔除内容：that 引导的宾语从句（内部嵌套 if 状语从句、省略 that 定语从句、第二层 that 宾语从句、that 定语从句）  
洋葱核：He argued.  
主干译文：他提出。

②洋葱皮解析  
1. that if we ignore the damage human activities have done to the ocean, we will soon find that many marine species that once thrived in these waters may disappear forever：宾语从句，作 argued 的宾语；
  - if we ignore the damage human activities have done to the ocean：宾语从句内部嵌套条件状语从句；human activities have done to the ocean：省略关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 damage；
  - that many marine species that once thrived in these waters may disappear forever：find 后面嵌套第二层宾语从句；that once thrived in these waters：定语从句修饰 marine species。

③重新组装洋葱  
第一层（主干）：He argued.  
第二层（外层宾语从句框架）：He argued that we will soon find something.  
译文：他提出我们很快会发现某件事。  
第三层（加入 if 条件从句）：He argued that if we ignore the damage, we will soon find something.  
译文：他提出，如果我们忽视这种破坏，我们很快会发现某件事。  
第四层（加入修饰 damage 的定语从句）：He argued that if we ignore the damage human activities have done to the ocean, we will soon find something.  
译文：他提出，如果我们忽视人类活动对海洋造成的破坏，我们很快会发现某件事。
